Support

Simple Scripture is a minimal iOS Bible reading app — no account, no subscription, just read.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I change the translation?

    Open the iOS Settings app, go to the Apps section, scroll down to find Simple Scripture. From there, you can change your translation setting.

  • How do I change the font size?

    Open the iOS Settings app, go the the Apps section, scroll down to find Simple Scripture. From there, you can change font size. The app is also reactive to the system font size option found in accessibility.

  • Does the App require an internet connection?

    No. All Bible text is bundled inside the App. You can read anywhere — airplane mode, no signal, wherever.

  • Does the App save my place?

    Yes. Your reading position is saved automatically each time you leave the App, and restored when you return.

  • Is there a subscription or in-app purchase?

    No subscription. The App is a one-time purchase of $0.99. That's it — everything is included.

  • How do I report a bug or send feedback?

    Send an email to greenguyapps@pm.me. Bug reports, typo corrections, and honest feedback are all welcome.
