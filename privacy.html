Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Simple Scripture (the "App") handles information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

Information We Collect

None. The App does not collect personal information of any kind. There is no account creation, no login, and no requirement to provide a name, email address, or any other identifier. We have no way to identify you because we never ask who you are.

Data Storage

Any data the App stores — your reading position, font size preference, and selected translation — lives entirely on your device. It is saved using iOS system storage (UserDefaults, within the app's sandbox). This data is never transmitted to a server, never shared with any third party, and never leaves your device.

Analytics and Tracking

The App contains no analytics, no crash-reporting services, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. We do not use advertising identifiers. We do not track what you read, how long you read, or any other behavior inside the App.

Internet Connectivity

The App does not require an internet connection, and it does not make network requests. All Bible text is bundled locally within the App at install time.

Third-Party Services

None. The App does not integrate any advertising networks, analytics platforms, cloud services, or other third-party libraries that collect or transmit data.

Bible Text Licensing

The App includes the Berean Standard Bible (BSB), used under its free license for distribution. It also includes the King James Version (KJV) and the Bible in Basic English (BBE), both of which are in the public domain.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected at all, the App is appropriate for users of any age.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is ever updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. We do not expect material changes given that the App collects nothing.
